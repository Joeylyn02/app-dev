# Our Beloved Summer 

*A nostalgic, slow-burn romance about youth, growth, and second chances.*

---

## Quick Facts & Trivia
* **Original Title:** 그 해 우리는 (*Geu Hae Urineun*)
* **Release Window:** December 6, 2021 – January 25, 2022
* **Episode Titles:** Every episode is named after a famous movie![^1]
  1. *I Know What You Did Last Summer* (Episode 1)
  2. *1792 Days of Summer* (Episode 2)
  3. *10 Things I Hate About You* (Episode 3)
* **Relationship Status:** ~~Broken up for 5 years~~ **Back together & married**
